# SunChips game design previews

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These are review boards, not Unity-ready textures. Each board explores a menu background, a roadside ad, and a collectible token. The selected direction should be rendered as separate production assets before it is wired into the game.

## Prompt set

1. **Golden Hour** — Warm sunrise over a stylized city running street, red and gold Garden Salsa inspired billboard with legible SunChips branding, and a golden ridged chip medallion. Keep the menu center clear for UI.
2. **Blue Burst** — Electric blue night city with lime yellow highlights, a cobalt SunChips billboard with chips and a sun graphic, and a blue-rimmed chip token. Keep the street and pickup readable at mobile size.
3. **Harvest Dash** — Clear turquoise sky, green hills, golden wheat fields, a road leading toward the city, a cream and green SunChips billboard, and a green and gold sun and chip token.
4. **Salsa Street** — Bright food-market street in tomato red, orange, lime green, and cream; red SunChips billboard with ridged chips and salsa ingredients; red and gold chip token.
5. **Sun Pop** — Graphic pop-art city with sunshine yellow, coral, cream, and teal; bold SunChips billboard with a ridged chip illustration; simple high-contrast token.

All five prompts requested a three-panel portrait mobile endless runner concept board with no Coca-Cola imagery. Some boards depict illustrative UI or scenery only for preview; implementation should preserve working game UI and gameplay geometry.

## Gameplay screenshot revisions

The `Gameplay` folder contains five additional built-in image generation mockups based on the supplied Unity gameplay screenshots. The prompts preserve the existing camera, asphalt road, lane markings, sidewalks, buildings, sign placements, and coin trail while exploring ad surfaces and pickup faces:

1. **Product First** — warm red Garden Salsa ad, product bag and ridged chips, matte gold chip collectible.
2. **Editorial Cream** — off-white printed ads, forest-green SunChips logo, one chip, minimal gold embossed coins.
3. **Cobalt Pack** — cobalt ads, white logo, yellow sun line, green footer, simple gold coins.
4. **Sun Yellow** — strong yellow ads with dark logo and red Garden Salsa bag, red-and-gold coins.
5. **Deep Green** — dark forest-green ads with white logo and single chip, brushed gold coins.

These are visual mockups. They are not texture maps and have not been applied to any Unity scene or prefab.
